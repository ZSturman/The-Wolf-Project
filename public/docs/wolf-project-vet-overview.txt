WHAT WE DO
The Wolf Project is a 501(c)(3) (in formation) that pays veterinary hospitals
directly to fund emergency care for dogs whose families would otherwise be
forced into financial euthanasia.

HOW WE WORK WITH HOSPITALS
- Vets identify treatable, time-sensitive cases where cost is the barrier
- Vets refer the family to thewolfproject.org/apply
- We review the case in coordination with the treating clinician
- On approval, we wire the deposit directly to the hospital
- The family receives private updates through a case portal

WHAT WE NEED FROM PARTNER HOSPITALS
- A primary point of contact for case communication
- A standard W-9 and remittance details on file
- Willingness to share de-identified outcome notes for transparency reporting
