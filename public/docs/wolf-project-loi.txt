To the leadership team at [Hospital name],

This letter expresses The Wolf Project's intent to enter into a working
partnership with your hospital to provide emergency veterinary funding for
dogs whose families face cost as the only barrier to treatment.

Under this partnership:
- The Wolf Project will fund approved emergency cases by paying the hospital
  directly via wire or ACH transfer.
- Your team will identify candidate cases and refer families to our
  application portal.
- We will share de-identified outcome data publicly to demonstrate impact to
  donors.

This Letter of Intent is non-binding and serves as the basis for a formal
partnership agreement to follow.

Sincerely,
The Wolf Project Founding Team
